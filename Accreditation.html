NABH ACCREDITATION

Labaid Limited (Diagnostic) (2023)

Labaid Cardiac Hospital is a single specialty hospital and the first super specialty private hospital of the country is dedicated exclusively for diagnosis and treatment of cardiovascular diseases. We are committed to ensure patient safety by maintaining the international standard. We are giving continuous training program to keep our every staff aware on process and hospital protocols. We are first NABH International accredited hospital in Bangladesh. We achieved this certification on 2019 and renewal certification on March 2022. Bangladesh top ranking Cardiologist provides their best effort to cure their patient with empathy.

Labaid Specialized Hospital is a multispecialty hospital. We have advanced technology and most renowned Gynecologist, Orthopedics, urologist, surgeon and other consultants working under the same roof. We undergone a thorough on-site review of the quality and safety of patient care provided and is committed to continuously meeting rigorous international standard. We achieved International accreditation for Labaid Specialized Hospital on April 2022 with full support of doctor, nurse and all healthcare providers. Furthermore, NABH surveillance audit team was visited our hospital to inspection of standard adherence and we had successfully completed this audit on Nov 2023.

CAP ACCREDITATION

Labaid Cardiac Hospital (2019)

Labaid Diagnostics is the first laboratory to get accredited by foreign accreditation body (CAP) College of American Pathologies at international level. The laboratory got accreditation in a span of 1 year with hard work of lab team and excellent support by Labaid Management. The lab has been audited by four foreigners from USA and lab has achieved 99.9% successful rate during the audit. All our tests are accredited by CAP in all the departments (JAN-2023). The lab also ensures accurate reports for our patients, clients and customers. The lab also has IQCs, Protocol, EQAS programmes from CAP.

BAB accreditation

Labaid Diagnostics is also accredited by Bangladesh Accreditation Board (BAB). It is a national Accreditation body. Only few labs in Bangladesh has been accredited by BAB. The Labaid covers most of the routine and special tests accredited by BAB. The BAB follows 15189-2012 standard for accreditations of medical Laboratory in Bangladesh. The Labaid follows all the protocols, quality procedures, IQC and IQAS which are defined by BAB is the 15189-2012 standard. All our laboratory reports carry logos of CAP and BAB.
